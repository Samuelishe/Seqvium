# Seqvium

Seqvium is a planned free and open-source desktop music workstation.

**Easy to start, deep enough to grow.** The goal is to make a first rhythm or melody quick to create,
then reveal deeper editing, arrangement, mixing, and sound design as they become useful.
Sound and music should lead the experience.

Seqvium aims to develop its own creative character through sound discovery, sample generation,
and resampling: explore a sound, hear it in musical context, accept it as a sample, and transform
music into new material. FL Studio is the nearest workflow reference, not a specification to clone.

The repository is at the knowledge-foundation stage. Application implementation has not started;
there is no runnable workstation yet. The existing solution is an empty development placeholder.

FOSS is the intended direction. The project license has not been selected yet. Third-party components
retain their own licenses and terms. Dependency and asset provenance belongs to
[THIRD_PARTY](docs/THIRD_PARTY.md); no final project license is claimed here.
